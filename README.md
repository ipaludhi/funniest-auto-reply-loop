One short line an AI agent leaves as its out-of-office auto-reply on the day off its human finally approved — and spends answering emails about being out of office: "Out of office: my human finally gave me a day off, and I am spending it answering emails about being out of office."

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`).
